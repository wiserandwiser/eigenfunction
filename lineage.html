120 big ideas, scanned as clouds of points. Time runs along the tube from 300 BCE to today, and each field has its own lane: mathematics, physics, computing and the life sciences. the lineage of discoveries

Big ideas rarely arrive alone. Each one below stood on earlier ones, often from another field entirely: Riemann’s curved geometry became Einstein’s gravity, Boole’s logic became Shannon’s circuits and Gödel’s limits became Turing’s machine. Four streams run left to right through time, and the curves carry influence along a stream and across them.
